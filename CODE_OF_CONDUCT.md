# Bridra community code of conduct

Bridra contributors and maintainers are expected to keep every project space
safe, professional, and focused on constructive technical collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect across differences in identity, background,
  experience, language, and viewpoint.
- Give and receive technical feedback about the work rather than the person.
- State assumptions, evidence, tradeoffs, and uncertainty honestly.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, embargoes, and requests to stop unwanted contact.
- Repair harm, correct mistakes, and help newcomers use the documented process.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or unwanted personal attention.
- Discrimination, demeaning language, sexualized content, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately misleading technical claims.
- Publishing private information, secrets, vulnerability details, or private
  correspondence without permission.
- Retaliation against a person who reports a concern or participates in review.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository Issues, Pull Requests, reviews, security
advisories, release coordination, and other spaces where someone represents
Bridra or Cluion's Bridra community.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a confidential conduct report, use the repository's private vulnerability
reporting channel and prefix the report title with `Community Conduct`:

<https://github.com/cluion/bridra/security/advisories/new>

Conduct reports are handled separately from vulnerability severity. Do not put
sensitive conduct details in a public Issue. If the private channel is
unavailable, contact `hugo@cluion.com` without sensitive details so a
confidential channel can be arranged.

Repository owners or delegated maintainers will review reports privately, avoid
conflicts of interest, give involved people a fair opportunity to respond when
safe, and protect reporter confidentiality as far as practical. Responses may
include clarification, content removal, a warning, temporary restrictions,
removal from project spaces, or revocation of maintainer access. Serious or
repeated violations may result in a permanent ban.

A person subject to enforcement may request review by a repository owner who was
not the original decision-maker, when one is available. Safety and confidentiality
take priority over public disclosure of enforcement details.
